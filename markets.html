How it works

Each market is a Yes or No question. A share pays 1 diamond if you called it right and 0 if you didn't. A Yes share costing 0.62 means the crowd thinks it's about 62% likely. Buy shares you think are underpriced, then either hold them until an admin settles the market or sell them back early at the current price. Every market is priced on its own, so a group of related markets won't add up to exactly 100%.
